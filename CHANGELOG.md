# Changelog

All notable changes to Espalier standard. Versioning per SPEC section 14.

## 1.0.0-draft.2 (2026-08-20)

Amendment to rule 6 (section 9), with the consequential edits it forces elsewhere. No section or requirement was renumbered. Requirements 8.2, 9.4, 11.1 and 11.3 changed; the Level 3 enumeration in section 11 gained requirement numbers that already existed; section 3 became normative and gained terms; `TEMPLATES.md` T4 was regenerated.

Changed:

- Section 9's title now reads "registration is the single declaration, exposure is a projection", which is what the amended rule says; the section number is unchanged and is the coordinate that is cited. The abstract clause and the Level 3 prose were aligned with it.
- 9.1 restated as the governed-projection form and split into nine numbered sub-requirements, so that every obligation it carries can be cited to a distinct number. 9.1 itself keeps the base sentence: registration is the single act from which every declared surface derives. The sub-numbers run in adoption order, not logical order, because numbers here are a coordinate system; 9.1.3 states the base obligation that 9.1.1 and 9.1.2 qualify.
- 9.1 carries its evidence inline, refutation first: derivation of exposure produced no significant completeness advantage in the twin trial (exp-2, state incompleteness p = 0.270, Cliff's d = 0.48; tasks fully complete p = 0.270), and the arms did not separate on whether a change failed loudly (loud tasks 6,0,0,0,0 against 0,0,1,0,0, p = 1.000, d = 0.04, with the derived arm's entire count in the one stream the study's audit excluded for a session that billed zero tokens and never ran). The rule rests on the measured propagation cost (turns and edit sites, complete separation, d = -1.00, p = 0.0079 each with all five derived streams and p = 0.0159 each on the four that remain when the flagged stream is excluded) and on the failure-mode split (streams ending with a silently stale served surface, one of five derived against five of five hand-wired, Fisher exact p = 0.048, exploratory, and computed under the corrected reading of the run's instrument defect 8). 9.1.8 forbids citing this evidence as a completeness gain or as a difference in failure rate. The earlier draft's phrase that the derived arm's failures were refused or loud was withdrawn as unsupported after the audit correction.
- 8.2 now requires, per declared capability, the set of consumer surfaces it is exposed on, REQUIRED even when empty. The derivation check of 9.1 consumes that declaration and previously had no requirement to consume.
- 9.4 now binds only where no declaration accounts for a capability's absence from a surface. A withholding declared under 9.1.2 or 9.1.7 and reachable at the protocol is not a red-list finding, so a conforming per-caller projection no longer red-lists itself at Level 3.
- 11.1 now requires a claim at a level that cites 9.1, covering a model-facing capability system, to be assessed against both companions at the mirrored level and to name the version and the claimed level of each; naming a companion version without assessing against it is not a claim under that companion, and below a level that cites 9.1 no companion assessment is owed. That is where the other claim-form obligations live.
- 11.3 now states that a sub-requirement of a level-cited requirement counts as enumerated by that level, so the inheritance of 9.1.1 through 9.1.9 into a Level 3 claim is decidable from the conformance rules alone.
- Section 3 is now normative as vocabulary and gained the terms the amended rule turns on: served surface and consumer surface, capability, registry, caller, policy, entry declaration, budget, governed projection, withholding, announcement, reachability gate, assembly point and session mount. It contains no BCP 14 keyword, so its normative status adds no requirement.
- Section 14 now states how a companion version pin advances and what happens to claims already made: the pin advances only by amendment, and an open claim stands against the version pair it named. It also states the precedence between the two versioning rules inside a pre-release series: a normative change increments only the draft identifier, and the accumulated release bump is applied once at release.
- The status-of-this-amendment paragraph now records that the Level 3 requirement list cites the same numbers while Level 3 rises substantively, because 9.1.1 and 9.1.2 bind through 9.1, and that a Level 3 claim made against draft.1 is reassessed before it is restated.
- The conformance notation now states the evidence-mark convention the numbered requirements use, so that a reader of a cited requirement can tell which sentences carry an obligation and which recite a measurement. It maps onto the companions' `[E]`, `[DI]` and `[R]` tags.
- `TEMPLATES.md` T4 Level 3 block regenerated against the amended section 11, and T2's surfaces row aligned with 8.2.

Added:

- 9.1.1, derive once at a declared boundary. The projection is derived at a declared boundary, which section 3 defines as the assembly point or the session mount, and is not recomputed per turn. Materialization of the boundary-derived projection is now REQUIRED rather than RECOMMENDED, with a stated acceptance test: every turn of one served session attributes to a projection carrying the same recorded hash. Evidence: the provenance validation, four frozen readings with every non-empty served line attributed (16/16, 14/14, 17/17, 15/15) and seven single-edit differences each classified into exactly its own class with two silence controls holding; an undeclared inlined literal surfaced as unattributed rather than being attributed to the nearest declaration. Marked as design inference, not yet measured: that a per-turn projection degrades routing or stability, and the same-hash acceptance test itself.
- 9.1.2, withholding is a declaration and reachability is a protocol guarantee. The withholding is announced in the served surface as a derived count plus the withheld names, and reachability is enforced by the host at the protocol layer rather than by the announcement alone. The second obligation now states the measured predicate rather than asserting a guarantee: the host does not accept a refusal from a caller that has not searched, and on declining a refusal returns one observation and continues; the gate's firing record is the conformance evidence. Evidence (exp-3): necessity, removing the announcement dropped out-of-scope routing from 21/24 to 9/24 and added 8 confidently wrong routes plus 4 additional false refusals for a line of 48 to 70 tokens; insufficiency, which is the refutation of a condition declared before the run: with the announcement served the flagship model false-refused 3/3 deterministically with zero searches after reciting the withheld group holding the answer, 9/12 against 12/12, while the smaller model never exhibited the failure; the fix, a byte-identical prompt plus a host-side refusal gate restored 12/12, fired 3 times in 72 requests all ending correct, and fired zero times on the 48 in-scope and distractor requests. The gate's known limits are normative text, not footnotes: the predicate checks that a search happened, not that it was relevant; the zero false-refusal count is partly definitional; the sample is one task unit and three firings. The strengthened predicate stays RECOMMENDED and is marked design inference, not yet measured, at the same strength in both documents.
- 9.1.3 through 9.1.9, carrying the obligations the earlier draft stated in one paragraph or in unnumbered prose: registration is not exposure (9.1.3); the manifest carries the per-capability surface set the check reads (9.1.4); the context window is a served surface (9.1.5); the projection input set is closed and is exactly five, the registry, the policy, the caller identity, the entry declaration, and the budget, each with one recording location (9.1.6); a withheld surface is withheld by declaration (9.1.7); the evidence of 9.1 is cited honestly (9.1.8); and the two companion specifications are invoked normatively, with a precedence rule for a conflict between them (9.1.9).
- Level 3 in section 11 now enumerates 9.1.1 through 9.1.9 explicitly, so that the T4 checklist is derivable by enumeration rather than by inference from 11.3.
- Normative references to two companion specifications, cited by their own titles, "Capability hosts under Espalier" (`CAPABILITY_HOST.md`, version 0.1.0-draft.1) and "Agent debugging under derivation" (`AGENT_DEBUG.md`, version 0.1.0-draft.1), each normative within its scope, invoked by 9.1.1, 9.1.2, 9.1.6 and 9.1.9 so that the reference is load-bearing rather than decorative.
- Informative reference [STUDY-inf] resolving the exp-2 and exp-3 identifiers to the controlled study record by repository, snapshot commit, and file path, with an explicit statement of what an assessor who cannot reach the record may conclude. The earlier draft's claim that `RATIONALE.md` carried this citation was false and was removed; `RATIONALE.md` predates draft.2 and carries none of the experimental evidence.

Evidence base for this draft: exp-2, the two-arm twin trial of a derived capability host against a hand-wired twin of identical observable behavior, 10 streams, 150 subject sessions, 15 capability-churn tasks per stream, one developer model; exp-3, the projection-narrowing test, 48 capabilities in 8 bundles, 12 single-answer requests in three classes, four arms (full, narrowed, narrowed without the announcement, narrowed with the host-side gate), two models, 240 routed requests; and the mechanism validation replay against the archived error set of exp-2, from which the provenance artifact cited by 9.1.1 comes. The mechanisms themselves, including the refutation of the prose-behavior lock as a staleness detector (about 8 percent precision, admitted only as a compound with an advisory semantic judge), are specified in `CAPABILITY_HOST.md` rather than here.

## 1.0.0-draft.1 (2026-08-18)

Initial working draft. Seven rules, five-type dependency taxonomy, three-level conformance model (Mapped, Checked, Derived), templates, rationale with evidence base and related work. Forged in a production reference implementation; independently cross-validated against the Cordis / DeepSeek Harness plugin model (see RATIONALE.md).

## Naming

This standard was published in draft form under the name "Dependency Map Method (DMM)" on 2026-08-18 and 2026-08-20. It is named **Espalier** from 2026-08-21; the descriptive phrase "the dependency map method" is retained as a subtitle. Section and requirement numbers are unchanged, so a claim made against the earlier name at a given version number is a claim against the same text. The normative reference tag for this standard is `[ESPALIER]`.
